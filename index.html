SentryWave Hotspot
WiFi hotspot billing · hotspot.sentrywave.com

Sell WiFi access. Get paid by mobile money.

A billing platform for WiFi providers. Customers join your WiFi, pick a package and pay with EcoCash, InnBucks or O'mari, and their token reaches your router automatically.

  • Customers pay on their phone with EcoCash, InnBucks or O'mari
  • Tokens are created on the router for you, and stock tops itself up
  • Works with MikroTik and Ruijie, even without a public IP

Dariro
Paid social media promotion · dariro.co.zw

Brands pay. Promoters post. Everyone gets what they paid for.

Paid promotion on social media in Zimbabwe. Brands put their song, show, shop or cause in front of real followers, and Dariro holds the money until the post is up.

  • A brand's money is held from the moment a promoter takes the job, and returned if nobody posts
  • Every promoter profile is checked: no bought likes, no fake followers
  • Promoters are paid to their wallet within 48 hours, and cash out to EcoCash
